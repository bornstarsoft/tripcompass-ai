Already picked a course? Decide [whether Kyoto belongs in an Osaka day](/itinerary/osaka-or-kyoto-day-trip/), or prepare a [one-area rainy-day plan for Fukuoka](/itinerary/fukuoka-rainy-day-plan/). Each guide explains what to replace rather than adding more stops.

Before filling the first and last day, make a [luggage, check-in and airport plan](/itinerary/japan-arrival-departure-day-plan/). Confirm the essentials, then leave room for one small meal or walk instead of another district.

If Todaiji is the reason to leave Osaka, choose [a Nara temple-and-park day](/itinerary/osaka-to-nara-temple-park-day/). It replaces the Kyoto or Umeda day, with a short park visit and no required deer feeding.

For a more personal Tokyo afternoon, swap a shopping block for [Jimbocho books and cafes](/itinerary/tokyo-jimbocho-books-cafes/). Start with one shop and let curiosity decide whether to continue.

Prefer a garden or an exhibition? Choose [Kiyosumi-Shirakawa with a cafe break](/itinerary/tokyo-kiyosumi-shirakawa-garden-art-coffee/) as another Tokyo half-day, with one main attraction and time to talk afterward.

In Osaka, try [Doguyasuji tableware and food models](/itinerary/osaka-doguyasuji-kitchenware-walk/) instead of part of a Namba shopping day. Compare a browsing plan with a workshop-centered version before committing.

Considering a day beyond Fukuoka? Choose [a Dazaifu shrine day or museum day](/itinerary/fukuoka-dazaifu-shrine-museum-day/), including when to keep the city plan instead.
