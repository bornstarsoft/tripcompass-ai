## The choices behind this itinerary

**One hotel, one main outing a day and time to return for an afternoon rest.** Ask your parents how much walking feels comfortable and which meal and sleep times they prefer. Age alone does not tell you someone's stamina or interests. This itinerary prioritizes a shared pace over the number of attractions.

Unlike the weekend course, Tenjin shopping is not required. Choose a park walk and reduce shopping, or skip the park if shopping matters more. This is not a route verified for step-free access, wheelchair suitability or a particular step count.

## Who this page is for

- Families who want a short overseas trip with regular meals and rest.
- Travelers who prefer exploring one area slowly to changing hotels.
- Groups who value time to eat and talk together more than collecting sights.

A long hot-spring journey or substantial out-of-town sightseeing needs a different plan. For a more active city weekend, compare the [Fukuoka weekend course](/from-seoul/seoul-to-fukuoka-weekend-trip/).

## Suggested trip length and daily route

**3 days and 2 nights leave one full day in the middle.** Compare flights that leave room for an unhurried meal and check-in after travel when possible. This guide does not confirm operating flights or seats.

### Day 1: Hotel transfer and the first meal

1. Check your arrival terminal and hotel address before departure, and share the transfer plan with the group. Airport arrival is not hotel arrival.
2. Leave bags or check in, then eat nearby. Expect storage or somewhere to rest before check-in only if the hotel confirms it.
3. Rest in the room after eating. Explore the immediate area only if everyone feels like it; skip the outing after a late arrival.

**An easy airport detail to miss:** official guidance places the airport subway station at the domestic terminal. If arriving internationally and planning to use the subway, account for the interterminal shuttle too. Recheck stops and routes in the [airport subway guide](https://www.fukuoka-airport.jp/en/access/subway.html) and [terminal shuttle guide](https://www.fukuoka-airport.jp/en/access/bus2.html).

### Day 2: Choose Ohori Park or Tenjin

**For a walk:** eat breakfast, travel to Ohori Park and decide where to turn back before setting off. Explore only part of the lakeside. A full circuit and castle-site visit are not part of the default day. Check seating and toilets using the official map and on-site information.

Have lunch, then return to the hotel. Add afternoon shopping near your hotel only if everyone wants it. The park's relationship to neighboring cultural facilities is described in [Fukuoka City's Ohori Park area guide](https://gofukuoka.jp/en/route/detail/43ac4ead-b709-4d00-98db-7fd88da6d885).

**For shopping:** replace the park with one store or shopping location in Tenjin and a meal nearby. Do not link multiple malls together. Keep the afternoon return to the hotel in either version.

**Left out:** Dazaifu or Yufuin day trips, a full park circuit and a mandatory evening food-stall visit. Do not add long transfers or late returns without checking that the group actually wants them.

### Day 3: A meal, bags and the airport

Use airline and airport guidance to check the required departure allowance, then work backward to hotel departure. Do not introduce a new attraction or restaurant reservation before the flight. Include time to collect luggage and wait for transport.

## Choosing a hotel area and room

Compare the **actual location that makes it practical to return and rest**, not just the best-known district. Use the [Hakata vs Tenjin hotel-area guide](/from-seoul/fukuoka-hakata-vs-tenjin-hotels/) for more detailed criteria.

- Check stairs, crossings and elevator locations between the usable station exit and hotel entrance.
- Confirm room count, beds, bathroom layout, noise information and cancellation terms. Separate a request from a confirmed provision.
- If booking two rooms, ask about nearby placement without treating it as guaranteed.
- Check whether breakfast timing and food suit the group, not just whether breakfast is included.

For mobility assistance, ask the airport, station and hotel about your specific needs. [Fukuoka Airport accessibility information](https://www.fukuoka-airport.jp/en/barrier-free/) and [city subway facilities](https://subway.city.fukuoka.lg.jp/subway/barrier/universal/) are starting points, not guarantees about every route's current accessibility.

## If it rains or someone gets tired

**Do not replace one park outing with three indoor attractions.** Reduce the day to a meal near the hotel and perhaps one indoor visit. Even an indoor destination can involve uncovered travel, closures or opening-time checks.

If the queue is too long, choose another restaurant in the same area. If the group is tired after the outing, eat dinner near the hotel. Agree before traveling that dropping the next stop is acceptable.

## Budget considerations

For a rainy outing, the [Fukuoka rainy-day plan](/itinerary/fukuoka-rainy-day-plan/) keeps a meal, one interest and a rest in one area. Check the actual access route for your family rather than assuming an indoor venue is easy to reach.

List return flights and baggage, all required rooms for two nights, airport and local transport, meals, connectivity and a contingency you choose. Do not assume one room accepts the entire family.

Leave room to change transport before committing money to optional sightseeing. If considering a taxi, check passenger, luggage and boarding needs without assuming immediate availability. Use the [Japan travel budget guide](/from-seoul/japan-travel-budget/) to match categories and units rather than relying on an example price list.
