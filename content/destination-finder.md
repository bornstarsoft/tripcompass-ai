TripCompass AI starts with the destination decision. The goal is to help travelers narrow a broad idea like "three or four days from Seoul" into a short list of destinations worth comparing.

## Get a Destination Shortlist

Use the [English AI finder](/#destination-finder) or [한국어 AI 여행지 추천](/ko/#destination-finder).

Choose your departure city, trip length, travel period, budget style, travelers, preferred region, and travel interests. Count arrival and departure days in the trip length; three days away does not mean three full sightseeing days.

## Compare the Reasons, Not Just the Score

Recommendations include reasons to go, best-fit interests, cautions, a budget label, and suggested trip length. Use these to identify tradeoffs. Scores are AI planning estimates, not verified ratings, and budget labels are not price quotes.

Before choosing, check whether your actual flight times leave enough usable time, whether a hotel fits your daily route, and whether the group agrees on the main priority. Search buttons open external results; they do not confirm a booking or availability.

Repeated requests may reuse a recent recommendation. When a request cannot complete, general starting ideas remain available. The finder does not look up real-time prices or confirm official travel rules. Read [how your preferences are processed](/privacy/).
